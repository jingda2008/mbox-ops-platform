03 / Gatherings & private events

Give a shared interest a place to meet

Fans can gather around music they love; colleagues can start an evening with a song. Private hire enquiries are welcome during the day before 20:00 and in the evening for team gatherings, discussion groups and small meetings. Availability and arrangements require venue confirmation.

Plan a gathering that fits your group

01

Team gatherings

Share your date, group size and preference for music or conversation so the venue can discuss timing and seating.

02

Discussion groups & small meetings

Explain your speaking, discussion and equipment needs. For quieter conversation, confirm exclusive-use times and sound arrangements first.

03

Evening private hire

Discuss music, food and drink, group size and budget; the venue will confirm availability and pricing.
